About Me

While my master's degree in informatics provides a good foundation in computer science, I thrive at the intersection of design and technical implementation.

I enjoy collaborative environments where I can learn from experts, and build technology with a genuine human impact, especially in high-stakes sectors like HealthTech, MedTech, Security, and FinTech. Guided by the belief that AI should enhance human capability rather than replace the unique human touch, I am interested in the ethics and security issues during this current AI wave. I'm particularly drawn to EU-driven initiatives in these spaces, where technology and policy meet to shape outcomes at scale. If you're working in this space, I'd love to hear from you!

I am grateful for the hands-on experience I gained at Usaneers. Currently, I am extending my UX and React programming skills as a working student at Monaco Friends and a mentor at TUDesign student club.
